Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Winklo is a set of quick solo mini-games (Zip, Path Words, Word Match, and Category Race). You do not create an account. This policy explains what stays on your device and what may be processed when the app loads puzzles or fonts.

Short version: scores and streaks stay on your phone. We do not run ads, we do not sell data, and we do not require sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Best scores, best times, and daily streaks are saved locally with Android app preferences. That information is not uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling Winklo deletes it.

Information processed over the network

When the network is available, Winklo may read public puzzle catalogs from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Those catalogs are game levels and word lists, not your personal profile. Google may process technical data such as IP address and a Firebase installation identifier in order to deliver that service. See Firebase’s privacy information.

If Firebase is unavailable, the app falls back to puzzles bundled in the app.

The app also loads the Lexend font from Google Fonts. Google may see your IP address for that request. See Google’s privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or account
  • No advertising identifiers used for ads (the app has no ads)
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone
  • No payment or health information
  • No sale of personal information

Children

Winklo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can stop network puzzle loading by using the app offline; bundled puzzles still work. You can delete local scores by clearing app storage or uninstalling Winklo. Because there is no account, we cannot look up or delete a cloud profile for you.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
