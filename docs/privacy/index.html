Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Winklo is a set of quick solo mini-games (Zip, Path Words, Word Match, and Category Race). You do not create an account. This policy explains what stays on your device and what may be processed when the app loads puzzles, fonts, or stability and usage diagnostics.

Short version: scores and streaks stay on your phone. We do not run ads, we do not sell data, and we do not require sign-in. We use Google Firebase for puzzle content, Analytics (product usage events), and Crashlytics (crash reports in release builds).

Information stored on your device

Best scores, best times, and daily streaks are saved locally with Android app preferences. That information is not uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling Winklo deletes it.

Information processed over the network

When the network is available, Winklo may use Google Firebase services:

  • Cloud Firestore — public puzzle catalogs (game levels and word lists), not your personal profile.
  • Firebase Analytics — product events such as screens opened, games started or completed, hints and resets, tutorials, and results actions. These help us understand how the app is used. Events may include a game identifier, scores/times, and similar non-identifying gameplay metadata. Analytics also uses device or installation identifiers provided by Google’s SDKs.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash logs and diagnostics from release builds so we can fix stability issues. Reports may include stack traces, device/OS information, and related technical data. Crash collection is not enabled for typical debug developer builds.

Google may process technical data such as IP address and a Firebase installation identifier to deliver these services. See Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

If Firebase is unavailable, the app falls back to puzzles bundled in the app. Analytics and Crashlytics simply do not run in that case.

The app also loads the Lexend font from Google Fonts. Google may see your IP address for that request.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or account
  • No advertising identifiers used for ads (the app has no ads)
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone
  • No payment or health information
  • No sale of personal information

Children

Winklo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can stop network puzzle loading by using the app offline; bundled puzzles still work. You can delete local scores by clearing app storage or uninstalling Winklo. Because there is no account, we cannot look up or delete a cloud profile for you. Analytics and Crashlytics collection is required for the app’s current design (there is no in-app opt-out toggle).
